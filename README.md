# Supermarket_Grocery_Sales
Supermart Grocery Sales Dashboard

**Project Overview**
Developed an interactive Excel dashboard to analyze grocery sales, profitability, product performance, discounts, and geographic trends.
**Key Performance Indicators**
Total Sales: ₹1,49,56,982
Total Profit: Sum of Profit
Total Orders: Calculated from distinct Order IDs

**Project Objectives**
Sales Performance: Analyze sales trends, category contributions, and regional sales.
Product Performance: Identify top-selling categories and high-sales, low-profit products.
Profit & Discount Analysis: Compare profitability and examine discount patterns.
Geographic Performance: Identify leading states and cities and locations with high sales but low profit.

**Key Features**
KPI cards and interactive dashboard visuals.
Sales and profit analysis by category, product, and region.
Monthly sales trend analysis.
Discount and geographic performance comparisons.
PivotTables, PivotCharts, and slicers.

**Skills Demonstrated**
Microsoft Excel, data cleaning, PivotTables, PivotCharts, KPI reporting, sales analysis, profitability analysis, and dashboard development.
